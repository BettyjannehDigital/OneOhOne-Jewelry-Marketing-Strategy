## Overview

This repository contains the full marketing strategy suite developed for OneOhOne Jewelry — a real, active waist jewelry brand founded by Betty Janneh. These documents cover brand positioning, email marketing campaigns, paid advertising strategy, and the overall marketing strategy presentation.

All work is based on a live brand with real customers, real Etsy sales, and real social media presence.

## Key Skills Demonstrated

- **Brand Strategy** — Product layers (core/actual/augmented), brand essence, positioning statement, ethics clause
- **Consumer Research** — 3 detailed buyer personas with psychographics, pain points, and message resonance
- **Email Marketing** — 3-email campaign series mapped to buyer journey (MOFU → BOFU), segmentation strategy, subject line optimisation
- **Paid Advertising** — Meta Ads strategy with audience targeting, creative brief, KPIs, and budget planning
- **Content Planning** — Planner board screenshots showing structured campaign workflow
- **SMART Goal Setting** — Measurable 12–18 month marketing goals tied to business vision

## Campaign Snapshot

**Email Campaign Series:**
- Email 1: Welcome promo — Code `WELCOME101`, 10% off first order over 300 NOK
- Email 2: Care guide — Educational content to reduce purchase hesitation
- Email 3: New Year seasonal — Code `INTENTIONS15`, 15% off, community UGC campaign (#OneOhOneIntentions)

**Paid Ads (Meta):**
- Budget: 700 NOK (100 NOK/day, 7 days)
- Objective: Website traffic & brand awareness
- Target: Women 18–35, Norway, interests in jewelry, African culture, handmade goods
